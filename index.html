FAQ

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Kaun si gaadi scrap karwa sakte hain?

Car, jeep, van, truck aur dusri gaadiyan jo end-of-life ho chuki hain. Apni gaadi ki details call ya WhatsApp par bhejein, hum guide karenge.

Kaun se documents chahiye?

Aam taur par RC, ID proof, address proof aur gaadi ki chabi. Exact list gaadi aur case ke hisaab se alag ho sakti hai, isliye pehle humse confirm kar lein.

Kya vehicle pickup ki suvidha hai?

Haan, hum vehicle pickup service dete hain. Location aur terms ke liye humse baat karein.

Scrapping certificate kya hota hai?

Gaadi scrap hone ke baad aapko Certificate of Deposit diya jata hai, jo proof hai ki gaadi legal tareeke se scrap ho chuki hai. Iske fayde scrappage policy ke hisaab se alag ho sakte hain.

Process me kitna time lagta hai?

Time gaadi aur paperwork par depend karta hai. Sahi time jaanne ke liye 9926856000 par call karein.
